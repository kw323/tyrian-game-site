PROTECT THE STARSHIP — FULL CAMPAIGN BUILD 1.1.0

This offline release contains the complete cumulative campaign build.

Included systems:
- 101-stage campaign with pre-stage interactive briefings.
- Story dialogue flow with keyboard and mouse navigation.
- English voice-over assets for briefings and campaign contacts (539 packaged audio files).
- Six weapon families with 25 progression ranks each and synchronized descriptions.
- Reactor burst and full-recovery lockout system.
- Distinct Raider, Military, and Alien combat movement and salvos.
- Boss combat archetypes, Sera ally behavior, Archon reactor systems, Meltdown finale, epilogue, and credits.
- Title screen, new/continue/save management, mouse flight, and customizable keyboard controls.

Offline runtime:
The Windows edition starts a local server on 127.0.0.1 only while the game is open. It does not require an Internet connection.
