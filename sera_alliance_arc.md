# Sera Alliance Arc // Chapter 8: FRACTURE LINE

## Status

This is a narrative blueprint for review before the final English dialogue is inserted into `CampaignSystem.ts`. Voice production remains deferred until the campaign text is complete.

## Core premise

By Stage 71, Sera has already fought the pilot, challenged the military chain of command, and helped the pilot in several isolated operations. She still does not fully trust the pilot or Dr. Naomi, but she has discovered that her prototype's telemetry contains a hidden command signature. The signature appears in the flight data of both experimental ships and in several supposedly unrelated alien attacks.

The military receives a sealed order to seize Sera's prototype and eliminate the pilot as a compromised asset. Elena is not the author of the order; her command channel is being overridden by a higher authority. Sera realizes that returning to her laboratory means surrendering the only evidence. She chooses to stay with the pilot because the two prototypes can cross the unstable hazard corridors together.

The ten-stage arc is therefore not simply "Sera becomes friendly." It is a forced alliance that slowly becomes trust. The two pilots begin by coordinating weapons, then learn to cover each other's weaknesses, and finally choose the same objective even when neither of them receives a valid military order.

## Visual identity of Sera's ship

Sera's ship must remain mechanically mirrored but visually distinct. The pilot's craft is a compact forward-pointing experimental fighter with a clean central silhouette. Sera's craft uses a split crescent hull, offset twin engine nacelles, a magenta-white reactor core, and thin cyan diagnostic lines. Its shield is an asymmetric crescent arc rather than a circular glow. When it activates a mirrored tactical ability, the effect is tinted rose-violet instead of the pilot's green-cyan palette.

Her weapons, levels, generator behavior, shield values, tactical ability and damage rules are still copied from the player's loadout. The visual difference communicates that the two prototypes come from rival engineering programs rather than implying that Sera has different game mechanics.

## Why the alliance happens

The alliance has four linked causes:

1. Sera's test program is scheduled for silent termination after its telemetry identifies the same hidden command signature found in the pilot's ship.
2. The military's new orders classify both pilots as contaminated assets, but the order is transmitted through a command layer Elena cannot authenticate.
3. A chain of late-stage hazards—micro-singularities, asteroid belts and derelict megastructures—is being positioned to force the two prototypes into predictable flight corridors.
4. Sera discovers that the bounty contracts, military patrols and alien attacks are being manipulated to make the fleet destroy its own experimental evidence.

The supreme alien ruler remains unseen and unnamed in this arc. Only an encrypted strategic signal, a distorted emblem and a few psychologically targeted broadcasts are introduced. The real identity and motive wait for the final chapters.

## Emotional progression

At Stage 71, Sera is present but defensive. At Stage 74, she is hunted by the same institution that once employed her. At Stage 76, she and Naomi discover that their rivalry was engineered by a third party. At Stage 78, the supreme ruler's broadcast turns the conflict personal. At Stage 79, Sera makes an unambiguous moral choice without being ordered to do so. At Stage 80, she commits her evidence to the group and becomes a permanent ally.

## Dialogue rhythm for the arc

Each stage should use four to seven lines before launch: Elena or Naomi gives the operational opening, Sera adds a tactical or personal correction, Ghost appears only when his information is relevant, and the pilot confirms or challenges the order. During combat, use one short reveal rather than a long exposition. After Stage 80, use an After-Action scene with Sera, Naomi and Elena; the supreme ruler should not speak directly in the closing scene, only leave an incomplete signal.

## Voice and tone targets for later production

The arc should balance operational clarity, dry humor and emotional pressure. Naomi's jokes should target Sera's ship design without making Sera incompetent. Sera should answer sharply and become warmer only after Stage 79. Ghost should remain useful and suspicious, never omniscient. Elena should sound increasingly honest as her authority collapses. The pilot should move from short confirmations to independent decisions, especially at Stages 74, 79 and 80.
